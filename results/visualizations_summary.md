# Experimental Visualizations Summary

- Daytime ambient temperature rising above 12°C triggers worker bee departures.
- Hive weight drops by 1.0 to 2.4 kg between 09:00 and 13:00.
- Brood core temperature (t_i_3) remains clamped at 34.8°C ± 0.3°C.
