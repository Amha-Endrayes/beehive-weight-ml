# Data Dictionary: BeeObserver Sensor Telemetry
- t_i_1..5: Internal temperatures (°C) from DS18B20 digital array.
- t_o: Outside ambient temperature (°C).
- weight_kg: Gross hive weight (kg) from Bosche H30/H40 load cell.
- weight_kg_noOutlier: Filtered cumulative weight with beekeeper intervention jumps zeroed out.
- h: Internal relative humidity (%) from BME280.
- p: Atmospheric pressure (hPa) from BME280.
